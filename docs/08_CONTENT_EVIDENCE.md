# Content Evidence and Editorial Rules

This document defines generic evidence handling. The public repository contains no school-specific source register or private research records.

## Evidence states

- officially verified: an authoritative source supports the exact claim.
- school-account reported: a school account published the claim; independent verification or completion may remain unknown.
- partially verified: some association is supported, but key details need confirmation.
- historical notice: an announcement or dated publication; it does not prove current status or completion.
- unknown: no reliable basis for stating the claim as fact.
- approved for publication: a separate approval state, not an evidence-confidence label.

## Editorial rules

Keep source publication date, event date, verification date, and approval date distinct. Preserve attribution and qualification. Never convert an announcement into a confirmed outcome, infer a current admissions or contact status from an old source, or claim a school relationship without evidence.

Draft content may be published only after current factual review, school/editorial approval, translation review where relevant, and media rights/safeguarding clearance.

## Source boundary

Consult authorized current evidence held by the project owner. The evidence archive is not part of this public repository; see [the exclusion notice](reference/LOCAL_RESEARCH_NOT_INCLUDED.md). Do not reproduce private records in public code, documentation, logs, or generated pages.
