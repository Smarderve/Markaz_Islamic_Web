## Shared design requirements

Use responsive editorial page structures, accessible navigation, clear source/approval workflows, semantic data tables where relevant, and meaningful route titles. Keep factual content in an approved content layer; do not add claims, dates, results, contacts, facilities, or school relationships without current evidence and approval.

Forms may be designed but must not imply message delivery unless an approved service exists. Gallery media requires documented provenance, reuse permission, and safeguarding review.

The local detailed page specification and content drafts are not included in the public repository. See [the research exclusion notice](reference/LOCAL_RESEARCH_NOT_INCLUDED.md).
