# Product Requirements — Markaz One

## Product intent

A single, visually outstanding **public-facing information website for MARKAZ ISLAMIC SCHOOLS**. Primary Education and Secondary Education are ordinary destinations within Academics. Preserve official school names and source identifiers in factual records where needed; do not infer administrative, legal, campus, admissions, or automatic progression relationships.

**Project status:** privately produced, unofficial prototype pending school authorization. No online publication or logo/photo usage authorization is implied by the existence of public school posts.

## Audience and primary journeys

1. Visitor exploring Markaz → school-focused homepage → About, Academics, Islamic Education, School Life and verified admissions/contact information. Primary and Secondary are ordinary categories within Academics.
2. Parent seeking Secondary results → Secondary or Achievements → examination-specific **2025 NECTA** figures and official source.
3. Visitor seeking an event → Events or News → date, source, relevant educational level when known, _announcement/completion state_ → related story.
4. Researcher/former pupil → History → evidence-backed chronology without invented founding year.
5. Arabic / Kiswahili speaker → locale selection → fully localized equivalent page with correct direction and navigation.

## Site scope

The **14** core routes: Home, About, History, Primary Education, Secondary Education, Academics, Islamic Education, School Life, Achievements, News, Events, Admissions, Gallery, Contact. Primary and Secondary routes are educational-level views within the shared institutional site. Add news and event detail pages, local search, privacy notice, 404 and language selector as supporting templates where warranted.

## Core functional features

- Sticky/transparent-on-hero responsive header as contrast permits, one institutional navigation system, accessible desktop navigation and mobile drawer.
- Animated design with a Markaz-focused cinematic hero, rich school editorial sections, scroll-linked reveals and approved image storytelling. Primary and Secondary appear as ordinary academic destinations, not a homepage theme.
- Published news with topic/educational-level filters, search, pagination, correct metadata and permanent article links.
- Events with date, relevant educational level when known, source and truthful status; past calendar date alone does not establish occurrence.
- NECTA result tables; figures accessible as semantic HTML, not just animations.
- Source-backed galleries/media if approved; lightbox keyboard controls, provenance and alt text.
- Admissions guidance under one institutional identity, with level-specific details only where verified; no fabricated or stale deadlines; simple verified contacts.
- EN, SW, AR versions for every public route, with full Arabic RTL and correct translated metadata.
- Direct-link loading and indexable server-produced HTML for approved production routes; private/noindexed staging only.

## Explicit exclusions (v1)

CMS, database, student/parent/staff portals, account login, fees payments, registration tracking, mark sheets, timetable management, email newsletter, student document uploads and general administrative dashboards. No backend service unless future approved inquiry form requires it.

## Forms policy

Contact and admissions _UI can be designed_, but cannot be falsely labelled working. A static build does not securely deliver messages; form activation requires an approved actual endpoint, anti-spam, retention plan, HTTPS, delivery verification and destination confirmation. Until then provide only approved real contact methods, or a clearly disabled/informational inquiry feature.

## Content integrity

Keep level-specific facts in a separately reviewed, source-backed editorial workflow. The private research library is not included in this public repository. Public content requires current evidence, attribution, school/editorial approval, localization review, and media rights/safeguarding clearance.

## Out-of-scope promises

Do not say “the most advanced school website in Tanzania,” “all admissions open,” “verified all events,” or promise official school endorsement. Website quality is testable; school endorsement cannot be inferred.
