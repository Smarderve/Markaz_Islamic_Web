# Product Requirements — Markaz One

## Product intent
A single, visually outstanding **public-facing information website** about **Markaz Islamic Primary School** and **Markaz Islamic Seminary Secondary School**. School public records suggest a related visual identity, but a shared ownership, campus or governance relationship is not confirmed. Do not falsely imply a legally unified institution.

**Project status:** privately produced, unofficial prototype pending school authorization. No online publication or logo/photo usage authorization is implied by the existence of public school posts.

## Audience and primary journeys
1. Parent exploring school options → introductory homepage → Primary/Secondary → Academics and faith-based educational identity → verified admissions/contact information.
2. Parent seeking Secondary results → Secondary or Achievements → examination-specific **2025 NECTA** figures and official source.
3. Parent seeking an event → Events or News → date, source, school, *announcement/completion state* → related story.
4. Researcher/former pupil → History → evidence-backed chronology without invented founding year.
5. Arabic / Kiswahili speaker → locale selection → fully localized equivalent page with correct direction and navigation.

## Site scope
The **14** core routes: Home, About, History, Primary, Secondary, Academics, Islamic Education, School Life, Achievements, News, Events, Admissions, Gallery, Contact. Add news and event detail pages, local search, privacy notice, 404 and language selector as supporting templates where warranted.

## Core functional features
- Sticky/transparent-on-hero responsive header as contrast permits, navigable school switcher, accessible desktop navigation and mobile drawer.
- Animated design with cinematic hero, rich editorial sections, scroll-linked reveals, image storytelling, interactive but accessible school explorer and refined details on all pages.
- Published news with topic/school filters, search, pagination, correct metadata and permanent article links.
- Events with date, school, source and truthful status; past calendar date alone does not establish occurrence.
- NECTA result tables; figures accessible as semantic HTML, not just animations.
- Source-backed galleries/media if approved; lightbox keyboard controls, provenance and alt text.
- Dedicated Primary and Secondary admissions guidance; no fabricated or stale deadlines; simple verified contacts.
- EN, SW, AR versions for every public route, with full Arabic RTL and correct translated metadata.
- Direct-link loading and indexable server-produced HTML for approved production routes; private/noindexed staging only.

## Explicit exclusions (v1)
CMS, database, student/parent/staff portals, account login, fees payments, registration tracking, mark sheets, timetable management, email newsletter, student document uploads and general administrative dashboards. No backend service unless future approved inquiry form requires it.

## Forms policy
Contact and admissions *UI can be designed*, but cannot be falsely labelled working. A static build does not securely deliver messages; form activation requires an approved actual endpoint, anti-spam, retention plan, HTTPS, delivery verification and destination confirmation. Until then provide only approved real contact methods, or a clearly disabled/informational inquiry feature.

## Content integrity
Keep school-specific facts in a separately reviewed, source-backed editorial workflow. The private research library is not included in this public repository. Public content requires current evidence, attribution, school/editorial approval, localization review, and media rights/safeguarding clearance.

## Out-of-scope promises
Do not say “the most advanced school website in Tanzania,” “all admissions open,” “verified all events,” or promise official school endorsement. Website quality is testable; school endorsement cannot be inferred.
