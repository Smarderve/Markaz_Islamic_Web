## Status rules

Keep evidence confidence, school/editorial approval, translation review, and media permission as separate states. Do not mark a record approved merely to satisfy a build. Do not publish unreviewed factual claims, contact/admissions details, or pupil media.

The current Phase 03 locale dictionaries are all marked `draft`; no localized route is approved for public indexing or prerendering. `MARKAZ_PRIVATE_PREVIEW=1` enables the nine EN/SW/AR sample routes for local review only. A normal build prerenders only routes in the approved-locale list. Do not deploy the private-preview build.
