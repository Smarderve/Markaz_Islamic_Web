## Mission

Develop an independent, unofficial informational website concept for two school identities according to approved phases. The repository includes planning documents and a Phase 01 technical foundation; neither establishes school endorsement, publication permission, nor a complete public website.

## Planning decisions

- React and TypeScript; static-first architecture.
- Fourteen main pages; English, Kiswahili, and Arabic with genuine Arabic right-to-left support.
- No CMS, application database, student/parent/staff portals, grades, accounts, payments, or document uploads.
- One UI foundation; selected motion components are candidates pending implementation tests.
- Motion for React is primary; add GSAP only when a demonstrated need and approval justify it.
- The provisional palette uses Markaz green, emerald, lime, yellow, and white. Do not redraw a school logo.
- Accessibility, performance, reduced motion, truthful content, and RTL behavior take precedence over decorative effects.

## Evidence, privacy, and publication

- The owner's current, authorized research is the factual source. Private research and media are intentionally excluded from this public repository. Do not treat this planning pack as verified school evidence.
- Never invent school history, leadership, facilities, contacts, admissions status, or completed event outcomes.
- Public visibility does not establish reuse permission. Do not publish identifiable pupil media or logos without documented rights and safeguarding approval.
- Do not include credentials, session files, cookies, private source registers, or local private records.
- No public release or publication without explicit approval.
