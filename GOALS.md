# GOALS — Markaz One

## Outcome
Create an engaging, fast, reliable and culturally appropriate school website that is **fully designed around coordinated motion**, with distinct but coherent Primary and Secondary experiences. The project is an **independent unofficial prototype** until school approval is obtained.

## Must deliver
1. 14 content-complete public page templates plus news/event detail, search, localized 404 and source-backed galleries where rights allow.
2. English, Kiswahili, Arabic: complete reviewed translations of every published UI and page, including metadata, article bodies, forms and errors; correct Arabic RTL and layout reversals where appropriate.
3. Custom Markaz green/lime design language, original typography-led cinematic hero, interactive Primary/Secondary explorer and meaningful motion across all pages.
4. Responsive and usable navigation, local-data-backed news/events/achievements, accurate results, safe gallery, search and admissions/contact information.
5. Static-first SEO architecture; semantic/accessible UI; fast mobile performance, reduced-motion and low-data fallback.
6. Source tracing for factual claims and content publication approvals; a safe content pipeline with no CMS/database in v1.
7. Automated and hands-on testing across locales, breakpoints and engines, complete deployment instructions, reversible releases.

## Explicit non-goals
CMS; parent/student/staff portals; grade management; registration backend; accounts/auth; attendance; paid features; payment processing; student-document upload; bulk Instagram scraping; photo fabrication; unapproved school branding/public release.

## Success measures
- Every published route statically rendered and directly loadable; no 404 on refresh.
- Every published locale/page translation complete and approved; no untranslated stray keys or mixed-LTR/RTL bugs.
- No critical functional, keyboard, contrast, motion-sickness or layout regressions in phone/tablet/desktop tests.
- Historical versus current events/admissions clearly represented; academic divisions match cited sources; unapproved media withheld.
- Core Web Vitals **targets** at 75th percentile: LCP <=2.5s, INP <=200ms, CLS <=0.1 after real-user measurement; lab baselines during development.
- Accessible according to WCAG 2.2 AA target (manual review required in addition to automated checks).
- Local development and static hosting remain low-cost. No required paid service introduced without approval.
- All test claims based on observed runs; any known fail documented rather than hidden.

## Gates
Architecture approved before dependency installation; three representative page designs approved before scaling to 14; current contacts/form delivery and media use approved before public accessibility; user approval before push/deploy.
